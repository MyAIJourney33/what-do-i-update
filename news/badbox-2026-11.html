Cheap no-name TV boxes used in record ad fraud

Heads up

Nov 19, 2026 · Might affect you

If this is you: If you have a cheap, uncertified no-name Android TV box: it may be one of the boxes used for ad fraud. Roku, Fire TV, Apple TV, Chromecast and name-brand TVs aren't part of it. Google says to check a box is Play Protect certified, and the FBI says to consider disconnecting suspicious devices.

If not: Nothing for you to do.

Source: FBI IC3 PSA on BADBOX 2.0, Jun 5, 2025; HUMAN Security via MarTech Cube, Nov 19, 2026
