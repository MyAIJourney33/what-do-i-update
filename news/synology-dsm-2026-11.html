Synology fixes two critical flaws in its home drives

Heads up

Nov 19, 2026 · Affects: Home storage drive (Synology, QNAP)

If you use it: Not a hack: Synology hasn't said anyone is using these flaws. It says to update DSM to 7.4.1-90080-2 or 7.3.2-86009-6 or newer (Control Panel › Update). On DSM 7.1 and BeeStation, where no full fix is out yet, Synology says to turn off the OAuth Service package.

If not: Nothing for you to do.

Source: Synology security advisories SA-26:21 and SA-26:20, Nov 19, 2026 (updated Nov 26)
