Hong Kong's Octopus card says hackers took customer data

Heads up

Nov 27, 2026 · Might affect you

If this is you: If you have an Octopus card or wallet in Hong Kong: Octopus says hackers got about 7,500 customers' names and photos, and some auto top-up bank details. It says card and bank account numbers weren't involved, and not to share details with messages claiming to be Octopus.

If not: Nothing for you to do.

Source: Octopus Cards Limited notice, Nov 27, 2026
